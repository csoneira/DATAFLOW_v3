# Change on calibrated holes

There was one of the gas inlets that gave a lot less of gas flux than it should.

What we did is changing the old one by a new one.

In the left of the figure you will see the change of inlet, confirming that is was not about the RPC, but about the gas inlet itself.

In the right we see how the flux stabilized in the four gas outlets after the old calibrated hole is changed by a new one.
